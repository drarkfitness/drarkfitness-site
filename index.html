What actually works

Diet trends come and go. Behaviour is the thing that lasts.

Keto. Intermittent fasting. Detox teas. Every year there is a new one, and every year people lose weight on it and put it back.

None of them failed because the science was wrong. They failed because nobody can live like that forever.

So a real part of this program is the boring, unglamorous work that nobody sells:

  • Mindset — why you eat when you are not hungry, and what to do about it
  • Goal setting — proper targets, broken into steps you can actually hit
  • Habit building — one change at a time, until it stops taking effort
  • Handling setbacks — because one bad weekend should not end the whole thing

This is the part that decides whether you are still holding your results in two years.

Client results

What real change looks like.

On Ozempic or Mounjaro?

The medicine is a tool. It is not the whole plan.

These medicines work. But two things go wrong for a lot of people on them.

You lose muscle along with the fat. When you eat much less, your body takes from muscle too — unless you get enough protein and train properly. You end up lighter but softer and weaker.

The weight comes back after you stop. If nothing about your eating and your habits changed while you were on it, there is nothing holding the result in place.

If you are on one of these, the program covers:

  • Getting enough protein on a small appetite
  • Training to keep your muscle
  • Handling side effects around food
  • Building habits that last past the medicine
  • A proper plan for coming off it

Your own doctor handles the medicine and the dose. My job is everything around it.
