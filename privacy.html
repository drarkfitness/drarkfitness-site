Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information Dr. ARK Fitness collects from you, why we collect it, what we do with it, and what choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Dr. ARK Fitness is operated by Dr. Abdur Rehman Khan (MBBS, RMP, PMDC Reg. No. 829867-03-M), based in Peshawar, Pakistan.

What we collect

When you fill in the form on our website

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your city and country
  • Your main health or fitness goal
  • What you have already tried
  • When you would like to start

If you become a client

Once you join a program, we collect a lot more, because we cannot build you a safe and sensible plan without it. This includes:

  • Your medical history and any conditions you have
  • Medicines and supplements you take
  • Family history of illness
  • Height, weight, body measurements and progress photos
  • Results of blood tests and other investigations
  • Your food intake, training, sleep, and daily habits
  • Messages between you and us

This is health information, and we treat it that way. We only collect what we actually need to coach you properly and safely. We never sell it, and we never share it with advertisers.

Automatically, when you visit the website

  • Basic analytics — pages visited, roughly where in the world you are, what device you used
  • This is used to understand how the website is performing. It does not identify you personally.

Why we collect it

  • To decide whether our program is right for you
  • To contact you about your enquiry, usually on WhatsApp
  • To build, review and adjust your plan if you become a client
  • To keep a record of your progress and of the advice given to you
  • To meet our professional and legal record-keeping obligations
  • To improve the website and our programs

Who can see it

Your information is seen by Dr. Abdur Rehman Khan and, where needed, by members of the Dr. ARK Fitness team who are helping to deliver your program. Everyone involved is expected to keep it confidential.

We also use outside services to run the business. These include:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — for messaging
  • Trainerize — for your plan, workouts and daily logging
  • Supabase — for storing enquiry and client records
  • Vercel — for hosting this website
  • Laboratories — where tests are arranged for you

These providers store data on servers that may be outside Pakistan. Each has its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your information to anyone, for any reason.

Your photos and your story

We will never publish your photos, your name, your results or your story anywhere without asking you first and getting your agreement in writing.

If you do agree, you can change your mind later and ask us to take them down. We will remove them from anything we control.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become clients: up to 24 months, then deleted
  • Client records: kept for as long as professional record-keeping requires, and usually longer if it helps us look after you in future
  • Health records: retained in line with medical record-keeping standards

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information — password-protected accounts, encrypted services, and access limited to people who need it.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong. If something serious happens to your data, we will tell you.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Stop contacting you

Email drarkfitness@gmail.com and we will deal with it.

If you are in the UK or the EU, you have additional rights under data protection law, including the right to object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Our programs are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top will tell you when it last changed.

Refunds and cancellation
